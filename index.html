Publication metrics

Themes are suggested from publication titles (with journal names as a hint) and reviewed corrections; they are an aid to browsing, not a formal coding of the research. JEDI Lab people (members, alumni and affiliated researchers, from the lab's people page) are matched to the bibliography's cited names by surname and first initial, plus reviewed spellings, so an unrelated namesake can occasionally be counted; their spellings are merged under one name. Other contributor nodes are cited coauthors. The source bibliography uses abbreviated author names and may omit authors where it uses an ellipsis. Publication data is maintained by the site administrator in the JSON dataset.
